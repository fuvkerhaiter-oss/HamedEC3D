## Why the score didn't move more

About 65% of the weighting is product/market and commercial readiness. Yesterday's fixes are mostly engineering, legal-safety and UX work, and they were the right ones. But the questions that cap the score are still unanswered:
- Do shoppers want a 3D store?
- Will anyone buy these products?
- Will anyone rent a unit?

The next real jump, to about 5.5–6, comes from the first paid orders and funnel data, not from more code.
